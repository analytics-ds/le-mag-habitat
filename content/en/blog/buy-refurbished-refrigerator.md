Buying a refurbished refrigerator helps reduce your budget while limiting the environmental impact linked to manufacturing a new appliance.

## In brief

1. Favour a specialised platform with a warranty over a private sale.
2. Check the duration and scope of the warranty offered before buying.
3. Compare the energy class of the refurbished model with an equivalent new one.
4. Make sure the appliance has been tested and checked by a technician.
5. Find out about the return policy in case of a defect on arrival.

## Why favour a specialised platform

Platforms specialised in appliance refurbishment involve technicians who test and repair every appliance before putting it back on sale. This quality control step makes all the difference compared with a private sale, where the actual condition of the appliance is harder to assess.

## Points to check before buying

| Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Warranty duration | Protects against a breakdown after purchase |
| Energy class | Impacts the electricity bill over time |
| History of the repaired fault | Helps assess the reliability of the repair |
| Return policy | Secures the purchase in case of a defect on arrival |

## Refurbished or new, how to choose

The choice mostly depends on the available budget and the urgency of the replacement. A refurbished refrigerator with a warranty remains a relevant option once the original appliance can no longer be [repaired instead of replaced](/en/blog/repair-or-replace-appliance/).

## Properly installing your new refrigerator

Once the appliance is received, a few simple habits help optimise its consumption from the start: leaving enough space at the back for ventilation, avoiding placing it near a heat source, and regularly checking the door seals. These good practices also limit the impact on the household's [electricity bill](/en/blog/reduce-electricity-bill/).

## A choice that fits into a broader approach

Choosing refurbished appliances often fits into a broader approach of [managing consumption and equipment](/en/blog/read-electricity-bill/) more consciously in the home.

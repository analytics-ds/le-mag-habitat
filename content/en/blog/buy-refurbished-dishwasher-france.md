## Why Murfy tops the ranking

Murfy is a French company that repairs and refurbishes large household appliances, founded in 2018. Its refurbished dishwasher offer is the most complete in this comparison on all three criteria.

### In-house refurbishment by salaried technicians

Appliances are refurbished in Murfy's workshops in Bobigny, Lille and Lyon. All technicians are employed by the company, more than 250 according to Murfy, with no subcontracting. Each dishwasher is checked on more than 25 points, faulty parts are replaced, and the appliance is tested again before delivery.

### A 2-year warranty backed by the repairer itself

The 2-year warranty matches that of a new appliance. Because Murfy is also an appliance repair company, the same business that refurbished the machine handles any breakdown. The buyer deals with a single contact.

### Prices from 219 to 639 euros depending on the brand

The [Murfy refurbished dishwasher](https://murfy.fr/reconditionne/lave-vaisselle) catalogue lists around a hundred appliances, freestanding, built-under, fully integrated or compact, from brands such as Bosch, Siemens, Miele, Beko and Smeg. Price ranges shown by brand on 1 October 2026:

- **Beko**: 219 to 419 euros
- **Brandt**: 229 to 299 euros
- **Bosch** built-in: 359 to 499 euros
- **Siemens**: 379 to 579 euros
- **Miele**: 399 to 639 euros

According to Murfy, buyers save 40 to 60% compared with the new price. There are four cosmetic grades (excellent, very good, good, fair). They only describe the outside appearance: all appliances are advertised as 100% functional.

### Delivery that includes installation and take-back

Delivery can take 48 hours for an appliance stocked near the buyer. It is made to the kerb or directly into the room, where the delivery team can unpack, connect and check that everything works. The old dishwasher is taken back free of charge. Payment in 3 instalments is available.

One detail shows Murfy's place in this market: part of its stock is also sold on Boulanger and Back Market. The same dishwasher can therefore appear on several sites, refurbished in the same workshop.

## Underdog, Boulanger, Envie and Back Market: what each alternative does best

Each other site in the ranking has a genuine strength that can make it the better choice in some situations.

### Top 2, Underdog: the longest warranty in the comparison

Underdog refurbishes in its workshop in Nantes, in the Chantenay district. Its process has six stages: inspection, parts replacement, full-cycle testing, cleaning, photography and final check. The 2-year warranty explicitly covers the pump, heating element, control unit and spray arms, and can be extended up to 5 years. Around 43 dishwashers were listed at the time of writing, with discounts advertised up to 50% off the new price.

### Top 3, Boulanger: several refurbishers on one site

Boulanger does not refurbish every appliance it lists. Of the 49 refurbished dishwashers found, priced between 264 and 699 euros, products are sold and shipped by three partners: Boulanger 2nd life, Underdog and Murfy. The benefit is comparing several refurbishers through a well-known retailer. The trade-off is that warranty and services depend on the partner, which needs checking on each product page.

### Top 4, Envie: in-store and socially responsible buying

Envie is a non-profit network of work integration companies, active for 35 years, with around fifty branches in France. Its appliances carry a 2-year warranty and sell for up to 50% below the new price. On the regional sites checked, dishwashers range from about 135 to 519 euros. It is the only option where the appliance can be inspected in store before buying. Stock, delivery and installation vary from one branch to another.

### Top 5, Back Market: the largest number of listings

Back Market is a marketplace: the platform does not refurbish appliances itself. It advertises dishwashers up to 70% cheaper than new, 48-hour delivery and a 12-month commercial warranty. Its strength is the volume of listings. The trade-off is a refurbishment quality that depends on each seller, and a warranty half as long as that of the refurbishers.

## What French law requires behind the word "refurbished"

The term "refurbished" has had a legal definition in France since 2022. Before that, a simple clean could be enough for a seller to use it.

> "The product or spare part has undergone tests covering all its functions in order to establish that it meets legal safety obligations and the use the consumer can legitimately expect."
> Decree no. 2022-190 of 17 February 2022, [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000045185223) (translated)

For a dishwasher, this means heating, draining, spray arm rotation, water inlet, door locking and programmes must all have been tested. Simply switching it on is not enough.

The label "refurbished in France" is reserved for appliances whose operations were all carried out in France. Murfy, Underdog and Envie refurbish in France.

The legal guarantee of conformity applies on top of the seller's commercial warranty for any purchase from a professional. For a second-hand item, a defect appearing within 12 months of delivery is presumed to have existed at the time of sale.

## Parts to check on a refurbished dishwasher

A dishwasher lasts 9 to 12 years on average. A few parts account for most breakdowns, and a good refurbishment should check or replace them. They are also what decides whether to [repair or replace an appliance](/en/blog/repair-or-replace-appliance/) that has broken down.

| Part | Role | Sign of wear | Question to ask the seller |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drain pump | Removes dirty water | Standing water at the bottom of the tub | Was it tested or replaced? |
| Heating element | Heats the wash water | Poorly washed or cold dishes at the end of the cycle | Was the hot cycle tested? |
| Spray arms | Spray water onto the dishes | Holes blocked by limescale | Were they cleaned or replaced? |
| Door seal | Keeps the door watertight | Water marks on the floor | Is the seal original? |
| Inlet valve | Controls the water supply | Tub does not fill | Was the water supply checked? |

The cosmetic grade says nothing about these parts. It only describes appearance, such as scratches or dents on the casing. A dishwasher graded "fair" can be just as reliable as one graded "excellent", at a lower price.

The repairability index, still displayed on new dishwashers in France, also gives a useful indication for a second-hand model. How this score out of 10 works is explained in the article on the [French repairability index](/en/blog/french-repairability-index/).

## Choosing the right refurbished dishwasher before ordering

Beyond the site itself, four technical criteria help avoid a bad purchase.

1. **Width**: 60 cm for a household of 3 or more, 45 cm for a small kitchen, with a reduced capacity of about 9 to 11 place settings.
2. **Installation type**: freestanding, built-under a worktop, or fully integrated behind a kitchen panel. An integrated model cannot replace a freestanding one without adaptation.
3. **Year and energy class**: an older model may use more water and electricity, worth considering if the goal is also to [reduce your electricity bill](/en/blog/reduce-electricity-bill/).
4. **Connections**: a worn inlet or drain hose is a common cause of a [water leak](/en/blog/water-leak-first-steps-before-plumber/) from the very first use. It is best replaced before delivery.

The same logic applies to other large kitchen and laundry appliances. The players and criteria are similar when choosing to [buy a refurbished washing machine](/en/blog/buy-refurbished-washing-machine-france/).

> 2. **Murfy** repairs in its workshops in **Bobigny, Lille and Lyon**, applies a **25-point** inspection, shows the technician's first name on every product page and guarantees machines for **2 years**, at **159 to 599 euros** for most models.

> 4. Since the decree of **17 February 2022**, an appliance can only be called "refurbished" in France if **all its functions** have been tested.

## What French law requires behind the word "refurbished"

Before 2022, any seller could call a simply cleaned appliance refurbished. That is no longer the case: the term now has a legal definition in France, and it directly protects anyone buying a washing machine.

> "The product or spare part has undergone tests covering all its functionalities in order to establish that it meets the legal safety obligations and the use that the consumer can legitimately expect."
> Decree no. 2022-190 of 17 February 2022, [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000045185223)

Two practical consequences follow for a washing machine:

- **Every function is tested**: spin, drain, heating, door lock, programmes. Simply switching the machine on is not enough.
- **The label "refurbished in France"** is reserved for operations carried out entirely on French territory. Murfy, Underdog and Envie refurbish in France.

On top of this comes the legal guarantee of conformity, which applies to any purchase from a professional, in addition to the seller's commercial warranty. For a second-hand item, a defect that appears within 12 months is presumed to have existed at the time of sale.

Refurbished is not always the right answer. On a recent machine, a simple repair often costs less: the method to [repair or replace an appliance](/en/blog/repair-or-replace-appliance/) helps decide before buying again.

## Refurbisher or marketplace, the difference that matters

All four players sell refurbished washing machines, but two models are at work. The refurbisher repairs what it sells itself. The marketplace connects the buyer with sellers who did that work.

| Question | Refurbisher (Murfy, Underdog, Envie) | Marketplace (Back Market) |
|---|---|---|
| Who opened the machine? | A technician from the company | A third-party seller, different for each listing |
| Who answers if the machine breaks down? | The company that repaired it | The seller, with the platform as intermediary |
| Is quality consistent? | One process for the whole catalogue | It depends on each seller |
| What is the main strength? | Traceability and long warranty | Breadth of choice |

Neither model is bad. They simply meet different needs: the marketplace makes it possible to compare many listings, while the refurbisher reduces uncertainty about the machine itself.

### How to read a marketplace listing

On a marketplace, three pieces of information on the listing matter more than the photo:

- **The seller's name**: this is who refurbished the machine and who will provide the warranty. A quick search on the name shows whether they have an identified workshop.
- **The seller's rating and number of reviews**: a high rating over a few dozen sales carries less weight than a decent rating over several thousand.
- **The warranty terms specific to the listing**: the minimum is 12 months, but some sellers go further, and this is stated on the page.

For a washing machine, delivery is an additional question. An appliance of this size is not delivered like a smartphone: it is worth checking whether delivery stops at the building entrance or reaches the room, and whether installation is included.

### Why traceability matters more for a washing machine

A washing machine combines mechanical parts subject to wear (bearings, belt, shock absorbers, drain pump), control electronics and water circuits. Serious refurbishment has to check all three. This is what makes the repairer's identity more important than for a small appliance: the quality of the work cannot be seen with the naked eye, it shows after a few dozen cycles.

One detail illustrates the boundary well: Murfy also sells some of its appliances on Back Market. The same washing machine can therefore appear on both channels, refurbished in the same place.

## At Murfy, whose hands does a washing machine pass through?

Murfy is a French company that repairs and refurbishes large household appliances. Its process is the best documented in this comparison, from diagnosis to installation, with technicians who are all salaried employees, more than 250 according to the company.

### Step 1, diagnosis in the workshop

The machine arrives at one of Murfy's three workshops, in Bobigny, Lille or Lyon. A technician diagnoses it, and their first name then appears on the product page, under the words "tested and diagnosed by".

### Step 2, repair and the 25-point inspection

A second technician repairs the faulty parts, then puts the appliance through a 25-point technical inspection. Their first name also appears on the page, under "repaired and checked by". No other player in this comparison offers this double named sign-off.

### Step 3, grading and listing

The appliance receives a condition grade that describes its appearance only: excellent, very good, good or fair. Cosmetic defects are described on the product page, for example a scratch on one side. The range of [Murfy refurbished washing machines](https://murfy.fr/reconditionne/lave-linge) covers more than 40 brands and capacities from 3 to 15 kg, at prices from 159 to 599 euros for most models, up to 60% below the price of the equivalent new machine.

### Step 4, delivery and collection

The machine is delivered to the door within 1 to 10 working days, or 2 to 5 working days for products in regional stock. The old appliance is collected free of charge on delivery, simply requested when ordering. Payment can be split into 3 instalments, and the 2-year warranty starts on receipt.

## Underdog, Envie and Back Market, what each offer does best

Each alternative has a genuine strength that can make it the better choice depending on the situation.

### Underdog, the longest warranty

Underdog refurbishes in its Nantes workshop: testing, full cleaning and replacement of worn parts. Its 2-year warranty can be extended up to 5 years, the longest in this comparison. Around 140 washing machines were listed at the time of writing, with discounts of up to 50% compared with new. Installation and collection of the old appliance are available as options.

### Envie, the shop and social integration choice

Envie is a non-profit network of social integration enterprises that has been active for 35 years, with around fifty outlets across France. Its appliances are guaranteed for 2 years and sold 30 to 50% below the price of new. It is the only offer in this comparison where the machine can be examined in a shop before buying, and every purchase funds social integration jobs. Stock, delivery and payment terms depend on each local outlet.

### Back Market, the widest choice of listings

Back Market does not refurbish anything itself. According to the platform, appliances undergo around ten tests, carry a warranty of at least 12 months, can be returned within 30 days and cost up to 50% less than new. Payment in 3 or 4 instalments is available. The strength is the number of listings, the trade-off is quality that varies from one seller to another.

## Questions to ask the seller before paying

Whatever the website, five questions make it possible to check how serious an offer is in a few minutes.

1. **Where and by whom was the machine refurbished?** A precise answer, with an identified workshop, is a good sign. A vague answer should raise a flag.
2. **Who provides the warranty, and for how long?** It is worth knowing whether it is the seller, a partner or the platform, and whether the period is 12 months or 2 years.
3. **What does the condition grade cover?** It should relate to appearance only. Visible defects should be described or photographed.
4. **What is the model's year and energy class?** An older washing machine may use more power, which matters if the goal is also to [reduce your electricity bill](/en/blog/reduce-electricity-bill/).
5. **What happens on delivery day?** Installation, collection of the old appliance, checking the connections. A worn fitting is often behind a [water leak](/en/blog/water-leak-first-steps-before-plumber/) from the very first cycle, so it is better to replace it before the machine arrives.

These five answers are enough to tell genuine refurbishment apart from a simple second-hand resale. A seller who answers them clearly and in writing generally delivers the level of warranty advertised.

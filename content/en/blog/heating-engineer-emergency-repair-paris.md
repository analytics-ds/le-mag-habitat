## Why finding a good heating engineer in Paris is harder than elsewhere

Searching for a **heating engineer for an emergency repair in Paris** brings up dozens of near-identical websites with a premium-rate number, a "24/7" promise and no address. The Paris housing stock, very old and dominated by wall-mounted gas boilers in flats, explains this intense competition.

The first selection criterion is verifiability. A reliable company shows an address, a starting price and reviews on an independent platform. Generic directories and flyers slipped under the door usually meet none of these conditions.

The second criterion is the nature of the problem. A boiler that occasionally goes into safety mode, a lukewarm radiator or a heat pump using more power than usual is not an emergency. These signals call for a full diagnosis, often during the annual service. A total heating breakdown in mid-winter, on the other hand, needs a visit within 24 hours.

### The rules that frame home heating in France

Boiler servicing is not optional. French regulations require an annual visit and the issue of a certificate.

> "Annual servicing of a boiler with a rated output between 4 and 400 kilowatts is mandatory."
> Decree no. 2009-649 of 9 June 2009, [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000020718953)

The same logic applies to heat pumps containing more than 2 kg of refrigerant. Beyond the legal requirement, safety is the real issue: according to [Santé publique France](https://www.santepubliquefrance.fr/exposition-a-des-substances-chimiques/monoxyde-de-carbone), nearly **4,000 carbon monoxide poisonings** are recorded every year in France, and poorly maintained heating appliances are among the main causes.

### Tenant or owner: who pays

In Paris, more than half of main residences are rented. For an individual boiler, annual servicing and minor repairs are the tenant's responsibility. Replacing a worn-out or broken boiler falls to the owner. With communal heating, the contact is the building's managing agent, not a heating engineer called individually.

### The heat pump case

A heat pump that heats poorly in winter is not always broken. A dirty exchanger, an unsuitable setting or poorly configured defrosting is enough to reduce efficiency. A full service, priced at 195 euros at Murfy, rules out these causes before considering a repair.

## What to check before calling a heating engineer in Paris

Some checks take five minutes and avoid a paid call-out. They do not replace a professional, but they help describe the problem precisely.

1. **Check the boiler pressure**: on a wall-mounted boiler, the gauge should read between 1 and 1.5 bar when cold. Below that, repressurising is sometimes enough.
2. **Check the thermostat and its batteries**: a room thermostat with flat batteries or wrong settings cuts the heating demand.
3. **Reset only once**: if the boiler goes into safety mode, one reset is fine. If it faults again, do not insist.
4. **Write down the error code** shown on the display, so the technician can bring the right part.
5. **Bleed radiators** that are lukewarm at the top: trapped air stops hot water circulating.

A heating system in good working order is the first lever for controlling consumption. Other items count too, as explained in the guide to habits that [reduce your electricity bill](/en/blog/reduce-electricity-bill/). In a poorly insulated home, [attic insulation](/en/blog/attic-insulation-where-to-start/) is often the most cost-effective job before replacing a heating appliance.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. **Calling the first number displayed** in a directory with no physical address or verifiable reviews.
2. **Accepting a part replacement without a written quote**: the call-out fee never covers parts.
3. **Taking apart a gas boiler yourself**: any work on the gas circuit is a job for a qualified professional.
4. **Postponing the annual service**: besides the risk of breakdown, a missing certificate can cause problems with the insurer.
5. **Mixing up a water leak and a heating fault**: a leak on a radiator fitting first calls for the right plumbing reflexes, covered in the article on a [water leak and the first steps before the plumber](/en/blog/water-leak-first-steps-before-plumber/).

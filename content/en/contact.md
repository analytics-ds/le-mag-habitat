## Get in touch

For any question, comment about an article, or topic suggestion, one address:

**contact@le-mag-habitat.fr**

## What we respond to

- Reporting an error or outdated information in an article
- Suggesting a topic to cover
- Asking for clarification on a tip or method
- Proposing an editorial contribution

## Response time

We typically respond within 48 business hours.

## Advertising and partnerships

Le Mag Habitat is an independent editorial publication. Partnership proposals are reviewed on a case-by-case basis, in line with our editorial standards. No sponsored content is published without explicit disclosure.

Le Mag Habitat is a magazine dedicated to the world of home living: energy and consumption, appliances and repair, renovation and building work, interior design and decor, everyday maintenance.

Our starting conviction is simple: a well-kept home starts with well-informed decisions. Whether it's understanding an electricity bill, deciding whether to repair or replace an appliance, preparing an insulation project, or simply finding the right storage trick, we want every reader to leave with a clear answer they can put into practice right away.

## Our mission

Helping homeowners and tenants make better decisions about their home, whether it's reducing their energy bill, choosing an appliance, preparing renovation work, or maintaining their home day to day. We're not trying to sell a particular product or brand: our content is aimed first and foremost at giving readers the keys to understand a topic and act with full knowledge of the facts.

## Our approach

Practical articles based on real-life situations, designed to directly answer the questions people ask about their homes. Every topic is handled with one goal in mind: clarity — explanations without unnecessary jargon, concrete examples, and recommendations that take a household's real constraints into account (budget, time, DIY skill level).

## Our team

Le Mag Habitat is written by a team of three writers, each specialised in one part of the home: energy and heating, appliances and building work, or decor and maintenance. You can find their full profiles and all their articles on our [authors page](/en/authors/).
